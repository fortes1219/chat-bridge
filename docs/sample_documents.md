# Sample long documents

Real documents published by Taiwan's financial regulators and central bank, shipped so the demo
can be tried on the kind of long Word and PDF files people actually have. The files are in
`data/documents/`, apart from the market events in `data/truth/`. This list is kept here rather
than in that folder, because `chat-bridge ingest` reads every Markdown file it is given.

Basis for republishing: Article 9 of Taiwan's Copyright Act
(https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=J0070017) excludes from copyright the
constitution, laws, orders and official documents, including texts drafted by civil servants in
the course of their duties. Each file below is such an official document.

| File | Document | Publisher | Source | Retrieved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fsc-ai-guidelines-2024.pdf` | Guidelines for the use of AI in the financial industry (June 2024) | Financial Supervisory Commission | https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL003920 | 2026-11-20 |
| `cbc-board-meeting-reference-2026-09-17.pdf` | Reference material for the press conference after the board meeting of 2026-09-17 | Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) | https://www.cbc.gov.tw/tw/cp-302-192864-4319f-1.html (attachment) | 2026-11-21 |
| `sfb-article-70-1-qa.pdf` | Q&A on Article 70-1 of the Securities Investment Trust and Consulting Act | Securities and Futures Bureau, Financial Supervisory Commission | https://www.sfb.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=1061&parentpath=0%2C6%2C858 | 2026-11-21 |
| `sfb-etf-qa-2003.pdf` | Q&A on exchange-traded funds (June 2003) | Securities and Futures Bureau, Financial Supervisory Commission | https://www.sfb.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=878&parentpath=0%2C6%2C858 | 2026-11-21 |
| `fsc-life-insurance-model-clauses-2006.doc` | Model clauses for life insurance policies, comparison table of amendments (2006) | Financial Supervisory Commission | https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FE060879 | 2026-11-21 |

The files are the originals as downloaded, unchanged.
