## 事件發生

- "The warnings intensified after Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay over the weekend calling for the industry to "pace the frontier"". [s1]
- "For too long the industry lied" about AI risks, Amodei told CBS News senior business and technology correspondent Jo Ling Kent in an interview. [s1]

## 影響股市證據

- The Nasdaq Composite fell 147 points, or 0.6%, to 26,186; the S&P 500 was down 0.5% and the Dow 0.3%. [s1]
- CBS wrote that investors "fretted over the sustainability of spending". [s1]

## 相關標的

- Nvidia slid 3.4% and Micron 5.3%. [s1]

## 後續與不同說法

- Adam Crisafulli of Vital Knowledge: "This pace of spending is absolutely not sustainable". [s1]
- David Royal, "chief financial and investment officer at financial services provider Thrivent", told CBS News: "Markets are trying to figure out what the pace [of AI development] is, and there will be winners and losers depending on what that pace is". [s1]
- Royal added: "I'm not too alarmed by what we're seeing today, but you are seeing some individual names in the chip space that are getting hit pretty hard". [s1]
- CBS cited a Capital Economics forecast of a more than 20% S&P 500 drop by end-2027. [s1]

> significance 依據（supporting）：AI支出能否持續的疑慮在市場上的一次具體反應，跌幅不在這段期間最大之列。
