## 事件發生

- China's additional 24% tariff on US imports "will continue to be suspended for two months from 1:01 p.m. (0501 GMT) on Tuesday, but a tariff of 10% will remain", its state council, or cabinet, said. [s1]
- The tariffs panel said in a statement that the move is "to implement the 'outcomes and consensus reached at the Washington meeting between the heads of state of China and the United States'". [s1]
- The suspension of port fees on US-linked ships "will be extended until January 10, 2027, China's transport ministry said in a statement". [s1]
- "China is also continuing to suspend a probe into shipping and shipbuilding supply chain security". [s1]
