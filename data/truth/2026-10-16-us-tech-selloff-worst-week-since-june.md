## 事件發生

- "A broad sell-off in technology companies pulled stocks lower". [s1]

## 影響股市證據

- The Dow "fell 175.28 points, or 0.3%, to 52,002.41"; the S&P 500 "fell 84.62 points, or 1.1%, to 7,659.44"; the Nasdaq "fell 572.99 points, or 2.1%, to 26,140.03". [s1]
- The article said investors worry that the companies "may not be able to keep borrowing and spending as heavily". [s1]

## 相關標的

- Micron fell 5.6%, AMD 6%, Broadcom 4.5%, Intel 5.8%, Meta 3.7% and Alphabet 2.5%. [s1]
