## 事件發生

- TSMC reported its third quarter 2026 results in a release datelined "HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C., Oct. 15, 2026". [s1]
- Fourth-quarter guidance: "Revenue is expected to be between US$52.3 billion and US$54.3 billion;" "Gross profit margin is expected to be between 65% and 67%;" based on "1 US dollar to 31.8 NT dollars". [s1]

## 相關標的

- TSMC reported "consolidated revenue of NT$1,494.24 billion, net income of NT$763.06 billion" and "diluted earnings per share of NT$29.43 (US$4.60 per ADR unit)". [s1]
- Year over year, "third quarter revenue increased 50.9%, while net income and diluted EPS both increased 68.7%". [s1]
- In US dollars, "third quarter revenue was $46.72 billion, which increased 41.2% year-over-year". [s1]
- "Gross margin for the quarter was 66.4%, operating margin was 58.7%, and net profit margin was 51.1%". [s1]
- "shipments of 2-nanometer accounted for 8% of total wafer revenue; 3-nanometer accounted for 33%;" "5-nanometer accounted for 28%; and 7-nanometer accounted for 10%". [s1]
