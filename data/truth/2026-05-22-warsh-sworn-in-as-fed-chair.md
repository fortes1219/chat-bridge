## 事件發生

- "Kevin Warsh on Friday took the oath of office as chairman and a member of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System". [s1]
- "Also on Friday, the Federal Open Market Committee unanimously selected Warsh as its chairman". [s1]
- "President Donald J. Trump nominated Mr. Warsh on March 4, 2026". [s1]
- "He was confirmed by the United States Senate to serve as a member of the Board on May 12, and as chairman of the Board on May 13". [s1]
- "His term as chairman ends on May 21, 2030, and his term as a member of the Board ends on January 31, 2040". [s1]
