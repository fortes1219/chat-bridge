## 事件發生

- "Nvidia said on Monday it would raise $25 billion through a U.S. bond issuance, as it taps the debt market to increase liquidity for the first time since 2021". [s1]
- "Investor demand for the bond sale hit $85 billion", according to one of the sources familiar with the matter cited by Reuters. [s1]
- "The AI chip leader has not accessed the investment-grade bond market in five years, previously raising $5 billion in June 2021, the source said". [s1]
- A spokesperson said proceeds are for general corporate purposes. [s1]
- "Big Tech companies have signaled that spending on AI would not slow down, with combined outlays set to surpass $700 billion this year, up from around $400 billion in 2025". [s1]
