- TSMC reported its second quarter 2026 results in a release datelined "HSINCHU, Taiwan, R.O.C., Jul. 16, 2026". [s1]

## 相關標的

- TSMC reported "consolidated revenue of NT$1,270.38 billion, net income of NT$706.56 billion" and "diluted earnings per share of NT$27.25 (US$4.31 per ADR unit)". [s1]
- Year over year, "second quarter revenue increased 36.0%, while net income and diluted EPS both increased 77.4%". [s1]
- In US dollars, "second quarter revenue was $40.20 billion, which increased 33.7% year-over-year". [s1]
- "Gross margin for the quarter was 67.7%, operating margin was 60.3%, and net profit margin was 55.6%". [s1]
- "shipments of 2-nanometer accounted for 3% of total wafer revenue; 3-nanometer accounted for 30%;" "5-nanometer accounted for 33%; and 7-nanometer accounted for 11%". [s1]
- Third-quarter guidance: "Revenue is expected to be between US$44.6 billion and US$45.8 billion;" "Gross profit margin is expected to be between 65% and 67%;" based on "1 US dollar to 32 NT dollars". [s1]
