## 事件發生

- BIS issued a rule addressing remote access to "advanced super intelligence (SI) chips and servers". [s1]
- The rule is "designed to ensure that existing restrictions on access to sensitive American technology cannot be circumvented through remote computing arrangements involving data centers in third countries". [s1]
- In another sentence, the release refers to "data centers located outside countries subject to U.S. export restrictions". [s1]
- The rule was "written by and at the direction of the Department of Treasury and the National Security Council". [s1]
- The press release links a Federal Register public-inspection document (2026-23529). [s1]
