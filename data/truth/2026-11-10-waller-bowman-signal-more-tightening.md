## 事件發生

- Governor Christopher Waller, speaking in London, said he would explain why he believes "further tightening of monetary policy will be needed, sooner rather than later" … [s1]
- Waller said he is leaning toward supporting a 25 basis point increase in the policy rate at the FOMC's December meeting, even if, as he expects, the monthly rate of inflation improves in October. [s1]
- Waller said that if the October CPI inflation data out later that day are similar to the past two monthly readings, with core CPI inflation around 0.3 percent or even higher, "a 50 basis point hike in the policy rate should be considered at the December meeting". [s1]
- Vice Chair for Supervision Michelle Bowman, speaking the same day in Washington, said "the benefit of additional policy tightening has to be carefully weighed against its costs for interest-rate-sensitive sectors". [s2]
- Bowman: "Financial conditions have tightened a bit recently because of lower equity prices, higher private interest rates, and a stronger dollar". [s2]

## 影響股市證據

- AP: the S&P 500 fell 29.01 to 7,520.93 (0.4%); the Dow fell 157.86 to 52,231.89 (0.3%); the Nasdaq fell 187.26 to 25,065.87 (0.7%). [s3]
- AP attributed the move to a report showing inflation was worse than economists expected, after which traders raised bets on coming Fed hikes; AP also noted a top Fed official had suggested a hike of twice the typical amount at next month's meeting. [s3]
