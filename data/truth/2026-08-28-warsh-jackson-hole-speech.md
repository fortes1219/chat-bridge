## 事件發生

- Chairman Kevin Warsh spoke under the title "In Our Time" at the Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City's economic policy symposium in Jackson Hole, Wyoming. [s1]
- Warsh: "We determine the path of short-term interest rates". [s1]
- Warsh: "Overall equity market volatility is low". [s1]
- Warsh: "Ever-expanding pools of capital are pouring into AI-related infrastructure of all sorts". [s1]
- As reported by Qatar Tribune, Warsh also said "short-term interest rates are the predominant tool" and "I would be hard pressed to describe broad financial conditions as restrictive". [s2]

## 影響股市證據

- "The reaction was stronger in the bond market following Warsh’s first speech as chairman of the Fed at an annual economic symposium held in Jackson Hole, Wyoming". [s2]
- Traders "upped their forecasts that the Fed will hike its federal funds rate as soon as next month"; they are "now betting on a nearly 58 percent probability of that, up from the 35 percent seen a day earlier", according to data from CME Group. [s2]
- The S&P 500 fell 19.23 to 7,711.76 (0.2%); the Nasdaq sank 138.93 to 26,402.42 (0.5%); the Dow dipped 9.45 to 53,559.99. [s2]

## 相關標的

- The yield on the two-year Treasury "jumped to 4.35 percent from 4.22 percent just before the speech". [s2]
- "The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.72 percent from 4.67 percent late Thursday" … [s2]
