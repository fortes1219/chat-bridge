## 事件發生

- "Eighty-seven percent of contacts surveyed" cited AI as a risk, up from 50% in May. [s1]
- Other risks cited: a broad risk-asset correction 52%, higher long-term rates 48%, cyberattacks 43%, fiscal debt sustainability 43%. [s1]
- The Fed report said advanced computing-related debt issuance reached $680 billion by October 2026, and that debt, guarantees and not-yet-commenced leases "could exceed $3 trillion". [s1]
- The Fed report said "The complex interlinkages among firms engaged in the data center buildout, public credit markets, private lenders, and special purpose financing vehicles create vulnerabilities" … [s1]
