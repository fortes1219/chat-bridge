## 事件發生

- The 10-year yield "touched 5.304% during trading on September 30, 2026, inching past the 5.303% intraday peak from 2007 and reaching a level last seen in May 2002". [s1]
- "The close was at 5.297%, up 4.2 basis points on the day". [s1]
- "The U.S. Treasury's official 2007 closing high for the 10-year was 5.26% on June 12, 2007" … [s1]
- The move came "the same morning the Federal Reserve's preferred inflation gauge cooled": core PCE was 3.0% from a year earlier in August, below the 3.3% economists expected. [s1]
