## 事件發生

- Al Jazeera reported that Meta raised its 2026 capex to $135bn to $145bn. [s1]

## 影響股市證據

- The Nasdaq Composite fell 2.6 percent and the S&P 500 slid 1.2 percent. [s1]
- Al Jazeera described "market jitters over the sustainability of companies' enormous capital investments in AI". [s1]
- The article cited Gartner estimates of global AI investment at $2.7 trillion in 2026 and $3.6 trillion in 2027. [s1]

## 相關標的

- Meta dropped nearly 7 percent. [s1]
- Amazon fell 4.6 percent, Nvidia 4.4 percent and Microsoft 2.2 percent; Apple rose 1.2 percent. [s1]
