## 事件發生

- NVIDIA reported third quarter fiscal 2027 results, for the quarter ended October 25, 2026: revenue "of $115.8 billion, up 20% from the previous quarter and up 103% from a year ago". [s1]
- Outlook for the fourth quarter (which "includes an additional week"): "Revenue is expected to be $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%" and "NVIDIA is not assuming any Data Center compute revenue from China in its outlook". [s1]

## 相關標的

- Data Center: "Third-quarter revenue was $107.6 billion, up 21% from the previous quarter and up 110% from a year ago". [s1]
- "GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share were $2.70 and $2.68, respectively". [s1]
- "GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were 74.8% and 74.9%, respectively". [s1]
- Colette Kress, "interim CEO and executive vice president and chief financial officer": "Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater". [s1]
- The forward-looking statements mention "expectations with respect to NVIDIA's leadership transition and the search for a new chief executive officer". [s1]
