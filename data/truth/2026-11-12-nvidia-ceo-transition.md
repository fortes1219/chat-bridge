## 事件發生

- "Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately". [s1]
- Colette Kress, "current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer", serves as interim CEO and keeps her EVP and CFO roles "until a permanent successor has been named". [s1]
- "The Board of Directors appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever Chair of the Board". [s1]
- "The Board is initiating a search for a permanent Chief Executive Officer". [s1]
- Harvey Jones: "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team". [s1]
- 「黃仁勳因涉及婚外情且有國安疑慮，辭去輝達（NVIDIA）總裁、執行長等職務，由財務長克瑞斯（Colette Kress）任臨時執行長」。[s2]

## 影響股市證據

- 中央社綜合外電報導亞股12日普遍收跌、投資人關注輝達；台北股市在「全數挫跌」之列。這篇寫於辭任消息之前。[s3]

## 相關標的

- 輝達股價「一度重挫逾14%，終場跌勢收斂，收跌4.3%」，「收在166.19美元，下跌7.54美元」。[s2]
